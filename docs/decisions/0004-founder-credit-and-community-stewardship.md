# ADR 0004 — Founder attribution without permanent network ownership

**Status:** Accepted for the research repository, 2026-11-10.  
**Decision:** Identify Christian Adams as Verunai's founder in the README, stewardship documentation, agent context, and project metadata, while committing to transparent, open-source, community-centered evolution.

## Context

The founder asked for a permanent, accurate historical credit. The project is also intended to outgrow founder-controlled services and avoid hidden premine or exclusive governance.

## Decision

- Record `Founded by Christian Adams (2026)` in discoverable project documentation.
- Describe the founder's practical role as initial organizer/steward, not the owner of all future network nodes, models, or participant contributions.
- Keep token economics, trademark rights, legal structure, maintainer succession, and protocol governance **undecided** pending public consultation, evidence, and legal review.
- Preserve all prior license notices and acknowledge contributions under the existing open-source license. Attribution is not a copyright ownership determination.

## Consequences

Founder identification remains visible as new contributors join, while governance and financial rights are not implied by documentation alone. Human-maintained decisions may evolve publicly as Verunai matures.
