# Verunai — Founding principles (draft for public discussion)

**Founded by Christian Adams in 2026.** Founder attribution recognizes the project initiator; it does not give the founder permanent control over independent network participants. See [project stewardship](PROJECT_STEWARDSHIP.md).


**Working name only:** Verunai. Brand and trademark clearance, domain and ticker selection are unfinished.

**Motto:** Built by everyone. Verified by anyone.

## Public-interest mission

Verunai is an open-source research effort toward permissionless, community-governed AI infrastructure. Anyone should be able to inspect the code, run a node, help evaluate the system, and—subject to real hardware requirements—compete to mine blocks. We aspire to public access to a source-citing AI knowledge service, with no permanent central authority controlling answers or the underlying ledger.

## What we intend to preserve

1. **Verifiability over blind trust:** State and rewards must be independently auditable, and technical claims should be reproducible.
2. **Permissionless participation:** No approval or exclusive hardware vendor required to join mining, within documented technical limits.
3. **Open development:** Public specifications, test results, security issues, development discussions, and transparent governance.
4. **Fair launch by design:** No undisclosed founder premine, secret mining advantage, or promised return; propose detailed issuance rules publicly well before launch.
5. **Open knowledge:** Work toward source citations, visible corrections, evaluation benchmarks, and safeguards against poisoning or misinformation.
6. **Independence from founder:** The intended mature network should be able to keep operating without its original author or a mandatory company fee.
7. **Research integrity:** Today's simulator does not demonstrate secure proof of useful AI work. Do not describe it as a production chain or profitable mining system.

## Immediate development priority

Demonstrate and attempt to break a proof-of-useful-AI-work algorithm. Show (a) AI usefulness, (b) miner work required, (c) verifier cost, (d) GPU performance, and (e) resistance to shortcut strategies. Negative results should be published.

## Community launch gates

- Repository openly licensed and independently reproducible.
- At least one adversarial review of mining and validation logic.
- Publicly measured mining and verification costs.
- Named, acknowledged limitations and research questions.
- Financial/token components **only** after independent security, legal, and economic analysis.

## Humanity-first safety and ethics

Public benefit, human rights, privacy and harm prevention take precedence over token value or growth. Read `docs/SAFETY_AND_ETHICS.md`. Open participation is a goal, not a guarantee that every fork, miner, or model deployment behaves ethically. Any public AI service or shared-model update requires documented evaluations and independent review; the present prototype meets none of those deployment gates.
