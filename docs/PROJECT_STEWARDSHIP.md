# Verunai — founder recognition and community stewardship

## Project founder

**Christian Adams founded Verunai in 2026** as an open-source public-interest research initiative pursuing decentralized, verifiable artificial intelligence.

Founder attribution recognizes the person who initiated and organized this project. It is **not** a claim that Verunai is currently an incorporated entity, legally registered brand, operational global network, independently audited protocol, deployed public AI model, or valuable cryptocurrency. Trademark/domain clearance remains pending.

## The founder's role during research

- Set the initial public-interest mission and engineering priorities.
- Coordinate the experimental repository, task roadmap, and review process.
- Approve changes merged into the founder-maintained repository until maintainership is broadened by a transparent process.
- Invite independent security review, correction, and outside contribution.
- Document conflicts of interest and any proposed token economics before a live launch.

## Long-term community autonomy

Verunai's *aspiration* is a permissionless system that remains usable without the founder, a proprietary gateway, or mandatory company payments. Repository maintainer privileges and the ability to control one's own hosted services are different from authority over independently running network nodes.

Future governance, maintainership succession, protocol change procedures, and any token issuance or founder reward policies must be discussed and published before public deployment. **No founder allocation, premine, sale, or privileged mining rights are established by this document.**

## Non-negotiable commitments

1. Humanity-first safety, privacy, truthfulness, accountable research, and accessibility.
2. Open, readable, modular, documented code and measurable performance.
3. Independent verification, honest disclosure of vulnerabilities, and security review.
4. Public contribution opportunities and fair treatment for volunteer developers/miners.
5. Founder recognition **without** unchecked permanent control over the future network.
